How to annotate genome?

You can provide your sequences by uploading FASTA format (.fasta or .fna)

Select tools using the checkboxes; all are selected by default.

Supported tools:

1. antiSMASH

2. Bakta

3. Prokka

After choosing your options, click Submit to start the annotation job.

You will receive a job ID and can revisit later if you do not wait for completion.
