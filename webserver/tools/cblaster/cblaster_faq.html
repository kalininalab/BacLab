How to use Cblaster?

Upload your sequences in GenBank format (.gbk, .gb, .gbff).

Select the blast type and database (default values are: Local & INHOUSE Myxo protein):

You can adjust parameters or keep the defaults.

Click Submit to start the cblaster job. You will receive a job ID and can revisit later.

cblaster results can be visualized on the results page or downloaded.
